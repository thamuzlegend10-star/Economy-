Economy

Run a corner shop. Order stock, fill shelves, hire staff, borrow wisely and grow your business district.

New game

How to play

  • Order stock in Procurement. A truck drives from the supplier depot to your warehouse. Cheaper suppliers are slower. Goods are only sellable after they arrive and are unloaded.
  • Restock shelves in Store. Stockers move goods from warehouse to shelf. Set an auto target to do it for you.
  • Customers walk in, browse, queue and pay. Empty shelves, high prices and long queues lower satisfaction and reputation.
  • Each day closes with rent, utilities, wages, marketing and interest, then a report that explains what changed.
  • Bank: sales land in the till as cash. Deposit it, borrow if needed, or an overdraft will cover bills at a high interest rate.
  • Modes: Standard ends after a set number of days with a score. Infinite never ends: bankruptcy gives you a recovery option instead.
  • Keys: Space pause, 1 / 2 / 3 speed, Esc close windows. Click buildings and objects in the world to open their tools.

Settings

Autosave runs every 20 seconds. You can also export your save as a file.
