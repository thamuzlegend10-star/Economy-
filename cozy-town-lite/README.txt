Cozy Town Lite — free 16x16 top-down town sample by Spaceyyy

One street to try Cozy Town in your game:
  tiles/{1x,2x}/town-street-lite.png   19 street tiles (asphalt, center line, crosswalk, curb
                                        ramps, sidewalk with curbs, grass edge); names in the .json
  buildings/{1x,2x,4x}/cafe.png          the cafe, by day (80 x 80 at 1x)
  characters/{1x,2x,4x}/player-1-walk.png  player 1, idle and walk in 4 directions (16 x 24 frames;
                                        rows: idle down/left/right/up, walk down/left/right/up)
  example-street.png                    how the pieces fit together

Cozy Town Complete has the whole town: 314 sprites, 293 tiles and 1104 character
frames (6 buildings by day and night, 3 interiors, street props, food, 4 playable characters with
8 animations, 10 neighbors), the 47-piece autotiles, Godot TileSet + SpriteFrames, Tiled files
and a Godot demo. Same world as Cozy Farm.
